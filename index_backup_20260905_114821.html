About LeatherBagsKingdom

Welcome to LeatherBagsKingdom.

My name is Alex Polak, and I create handcrafted leather goods in Ukraine using premium Italian leather and traditional craftsmanship.

Every wallet, bag, and EDC organizer is carefully made by hand with attention to every detail. My goal is to create products that combine timeless design, durability, and everyday functionality.

Thank you for visiting my workshop and supporting handmade craftsmanship.
