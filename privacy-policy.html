LeatherBagsKingdom Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Access

LeatherBagsKingdom may access information from a Pinterest account when the account owner authorizes our application to use Pinterest services. This may include information necessary to create, schedule, publish, and manage Pins and boards.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through Pinterest is used only to provide the requested Pinterest publishing and content-management functionality. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

3. Pinterest Content

The application may process images, titles, descriptions, links, and other content supplied by the account owner for creating and publishing Pins on Pinterest.

4. Authentication and Security

We do not intentionally store Pinterest account passwords. Authentication information and access tokens, when required, are handled only as necessary to provide the requested service.

5. Third-Party Services

The application may interact with Pinterest and Etsy when necessary to provide its functionality. These services have their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

7. User Control

The account owner may stop using the application and revoke its access through Pinterest account settings.

8. Children's Privacy

The application is not intended for children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or LeatherBagsKingdom, please contact us through our Etsy shop: LeatherBagsKingdom on Etsy
