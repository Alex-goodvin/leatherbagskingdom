Handmade Leather EDC

Keep your everyday essentials close while leaving your hands free with this handmade leather belt card holder. Wet-molded from Crazy Horse leather, it combines a slim profile with practical capacity for cards, folded cash, or other small EDC items.

The pouch measures 2.75 inches (70 mm) high, 4.33 inches (110 mm) wide, and 0.79 inches (20 mm) deep. Its integrated belt loop fits standard belts up to 1.57 inches wide (up to 40 mm), allowing the case to sit securely and comfortably at your waist.

As a minimalist wallet, it holds up to 10 plastic cards and 5–7 folded bills. It can also carry small daily essentials such as a classic lighter, including a Zippo, a small pocket knife, a multi-tool, or key fobs.

The pouch is hand-cut, wet-molded, and stitched with heavy-duty thread. A classic antique brass snap button helps keep the contents secure, while the slim construction sits close to the body without unnecessary bulk. Crazy Horse leather develops character through use, with scratches and wear contributing to a rich patina over time.

A practical handmade gift for husbands, fathers, travelers, and anyone who values compact everyday carry.
