Handmade Leather Bag

Designed for practical everyday carry, this small leather messenger bag combines a compact profile with useful organization. It is made from dense genuine leather and finished with durable hand saddle stitching for a handcrafted look and lasting strength.

Despite its small size, the bag is comfortable and spacious enough for everyday essentials such as pens, a wallet, keys and other small accessories. The lined interior includes two pockets, including one with a YKK zipper, plus a dedicated key holder to help keep smaller items organized.

The adjustable shoulder strap uses a buckle and extends up to 55.11 in (max length 140 cm), allowing the bag to be worn comfortably across the body or over the shoulder. Its versatile satchel design works with a range of clothing styles and makes a thoughtful gift for a husband, brother, father or loved one.
