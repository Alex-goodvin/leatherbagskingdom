📏 PERFECT FIT GUARANTEE
Shopping for a belt online can be stressful. We made it foolproof.
Please do NOT just order your pants size, and do NOT measure your old belt from end to end. Follow this simple 10-second trick for a tailored fit:
Grab your current favorite belt.
Measure from the hole you actually use to the very end of the leather where it meets the buckle.
That number is your belt size! Choose it from our options.
