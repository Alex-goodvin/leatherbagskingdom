Handmade Leather Belt

Built for everyday wear, this heavy-duty black leather belt combines substantial full-grain construction with a clean, classic design. It is handcrafted from 4 mm-thick premium Crust leather—approximately 10 oz in leather weight—and made as a single-layer strap designed to resist stretching, tearing, rolling, and sagging.

The 40 mm width is suited to denim, canvas, and other heavy casual trousers, filling standard belt loops with a sturdy, substantial feel. A minimalist heavy-duty Italian buckle provides a dependable finish, while metal rivets and traditional hand-stitching reinforce the buckle attachment. The edges are burnished and hand-painted for a smooth finish through your belt loops.

With regular wear, the leather is designed to develop a rich, rugged patina that reflects your personal use over time. It is a practical choice for everyday jeans and a durable gift for a husband, father, or boyfriend.

For the best fit, do not order based only on your trouser size and do not measure an old belt from end to end. Instead, measure from the hole you currently use to the end of the leather where it meets the buckle. Choose that measurement from the available belt-size options. The belt has 6 holes spaced 1.29 inches apart, so your measurement should place you at the middle hole with adjustment room in either direction.
