Handmade Leather Bag

This handmade leather crossbody bag combines compact everyday storage with comfortable hands-free carry. Despite its small size, it is designed to hold essentials such as pens, a wallet, keys and other small accessories.

The bag is made from Crazy Horse cattle leather with a distinctive waxed finish. Flame-retardant waxes give the leather a degree of water resistance, while its soft feel develops a beautiful patina over time. The result is a practical accessory with a classic, timeless character.

Inside, the bag is lined and includes two pockets, including one YKK zipper pocket, plus a dedicated key holder to help keep small essentials organized. The shoulder strap is adjustable with a buckle and extends up to 55.11" (maximum length 140 cm).

Dimensions: 10.23" × 8.66" × 2.36" (26 cm × 22 cm × 6 cm).

Its compact proportions and refined leather construction make it suitable for everyday use and a thoughtful gift for a loved one, husband, brother or father.
