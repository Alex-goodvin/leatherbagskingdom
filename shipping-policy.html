Shipping Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Privacy Policy  |  Shipping Policy  |  Refund Policy  |  User Agreement

1. Worldwide Shipping

LeatherBagsKingdom ships worldwide using Ukrposhta.

Shipping cost depends on the product, destination, package size, and shipping service selected or available for the order. Shipping is therefore not a fixed amount for all products and destinations.

2. Processing Time

Most products are ready to ship. Orders are normally processed within 1–3 business days.

3. Estimated Delivery Times

  • Europe: approximately 7–14 business days
  • United States and Canada: approximately 12–18 business days
  • Other destinations: approximately 14–30 business days

Delivery estimates are approximate and may be affected by postal operations, customs procedures, weather, holidays, or other circumstances outside our control.

4. Tracking

A tracking number is provided for shipped orders when tracking is available. Customers can use the tracking information to follow the shipment through the postal carrier.

5. Customs, Duties and Taxes

Where available, LeatherBagsKingdom uses shipping arrangements intended to provide Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) service.

Under these arrangements, applicable import duties and taxes are generally handled as part of the shipping process. Customs requirements vary by destination country, and LeatherBagsKingdom cannot guarantee that a shipment will never be subject to additional customs requirements, delays, inspections, or exceptional charges.

6. Lost or Damaged Shipments

If a shipment is confirmed as lost or arrives damaged, please contact LeatherBagsKingdom through the Contact section and provide the order details and any relevant photographs or tracking information.

We will investigate the situation and intend to provide an appropriate resolution, which may include a refund of the order value.

7. Incorrect or Incomplete Address

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address.

If a package is returned to Ukraine because of an incorrect or incomplete address, the order amount may be refunded after the returned package is received. Original shipping costs may not be refundable when the return is caused by an incorrect or incomplete address.

Reshipping may be possible, subject to availability and payment of the additional shipping cost.

8. Contact

If you have questions about shipping or delivery, please contact LeatherBagsKingdom through the Contact section of the website.
